Before you book

Questions we get.

Who do you work with?

Coaches, consultants and creators selling a high-ticket offer who already get some attention online — but don't have a reliable path from follower to client.

Do I need to be on camera?

Yes — you are the brand. We write, script and plan everything; you show up and film.

How much of my time does it take?

About two hours a week: filming, a short check-in form, and approving content within 48 hours.

How long until I see results?

Leads usually start around week three, once the funnel is live and content is feeding it. First sales normally land in weeks four to six.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Funnels, scripts, calendars and trackers are built inside your own accounts and stay there.

How are you paid?

A setup fee plus a share of revenue — on purpose. We only win properly when you do.
